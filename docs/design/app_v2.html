        9Sig · fixed strategy, read-only ISA · Trading 212

        9Sig · version nine-sig@1

        Latest version
        Running in Trading 212 · Stocks & Shares ISA since 3 Nov 2016. The account can’t be changed here; to run 9Sig in another account (e.g. a GIA), use Add strategy. It becomes a separate strategy with its own instruments, ledger and history.
        How it works

        Hold 60% in a leveraged Nasdaq-100 fund and 40% in a short-term bond fund. Each quarter a “signal line” for the growth fund rises 9%, plus half of any new money you added. If the growth fund is above the line you move the excess to safe; if below, you buy the shortfall from safe. Extreme falls pause selling.

        1. Signal line: last quarter’s line × 1.09, plus half of the contributions made during the quarter.
        2. Rebalance: only on completed calendar quarters, using verified quarter-end closes and your recorded holdings.
        3. Buying throttle: normal buys are funded in full below 90% of cash + bonds; larger buys are capped at 81% of buying power.
        4. 30 Down: if SPY closes ≤70% of its highest close in the previous 8 quarters, the next two sells are skipped; a reset to 60/40 is reviewed after.
        5. 100%-up spike: if TQQQ doubles within a quarter you get an urgent alert to review a reset.
        6. Never automatic: the app never trades. You trade manually and confirm what really happened.
