# Compliance scaffolding

These documents are **templates and descriptions of what the software does**. They are not legal advice. Whether and how the service must be registered, what it may say, how it is taxed, and what data-protection duties apply are the operator's to decide with their own advisers (see `CLAUDE.md`).

- `RETENTION.md` — what is kept, for how long, and what closing an account removes.
- `DPA_TEMPLATE.md` — a starting point if you act as processor for an organisation.
- `METHODOLOGY.md` — how results are produced and what they are not.
- Public pages: `/terms/`, `/privacy/`, `/.well-known/security.txt` (needs `CONTACT_EMAIL`).
- Optional behaviour: `REQUIRE_CHOICE_ACK=1` asks each person to tick that they chose their strategy themselves when adding one (off by default, so today's flow is unchanged).
