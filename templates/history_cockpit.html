Why are some returns unavailable?

Money-weighted XIRR needs recorded funding events and an independently supported GBP closing portfolio value. A simple cumulative percentage is only shown for one initial deposit without later external cash flows. Time-weighted returns need verified pre- and post-contribution valuations; daily balances alone cannot prove those returns. Drawdown is only calculated over an eligible, flow-safe return window.
