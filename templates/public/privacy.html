Privacy

The public site collects only what you choose to give it: your email address if you ask for an invite, or a username, email address and password if you create an account.

What we collect and why

  • Your email address, if you submit the invite form. It's used for one thing: to send you an invite link when there's a place. It's never sold, shared for marketing or added to a newsletter.
  • A spam check. The invite form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. Cloudflare processes technical details such as your IP address to do that, under its own privacy policy.
  • A security cookie. The form sets a small cookie that protects it from forged submissions. It's strictly necessary, holds nothing about you, and there are no advertising or analytics cookies.

How long it's kept

Your email is kept only while it's needed to send your invite, and is deleted whenever you ask.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted at any time. {% if contact_email %}Write to {{ contact_email }}.{% else %}Reply to any email you've had from Wealtharr.{% endif %} If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Your account

An account holds what you record (deposits, trades, reviews you confirm), your alert settings and, if you connect them, destinations for alerts such as an email address, a Discord or Telegram chat, or a webhook (stored encrypted). This is used only to run your strategies and send the alerts you chose. Other users cannot see it. Wealtharr never connects to a broker.

Who else handles data

  • Email and chat delivery: alerts and account emails go through the mail server the operator configured, and through Discord or Telegram if you connect them.
  • Prices: public price feeds are asked for market prices only; no personal data is sent.
  • Payments, if a paid plan is offered: Stripe or PayPal handle card and payment details; Wealtharr does not see or store them, and keeps only a customer reference.
  • Hosting: the operator's own servers and backups.

Support access

The operator can look at counts and settings of your account (never amounts, ledger rows or messages) only for the time you allow it, and every look is listed for you.

Closing your account

You can download your data and close your account yourself. Closing removes your name, email, alert destinations, saved connections and authenticator, and makes the account unusable. Your recorded ledger and reviews are append-only financial records; they stay without your name for the retention period in the operator's retention policy unless you ask for them to be erased.
