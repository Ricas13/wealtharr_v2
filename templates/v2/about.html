What this is

Wealtharr is a private tracker and calculator. You choose a published strategy. It applies that strategy's fixed rules to the deposits, trades and prices you record, and shows the result with the working. It is the job a configured spreadsheet does, kept tidy.

What it never does

  • It never connects to a broker, places an order, or moves money.
  • It never gives financial advice, checks that a strategy suits you, or says what you should do. It shows what the rules calculate.
  • It never invents a price or a trade. If a price is missing, stale or unchecked, it shows nothing instead of guessing.

What you do

  1. Record the money you put in, and the trades you actually make, with the figures your broker gave you.
  2. When the rules output is ready, compare it with live prices at your broker.
  3. Decide for yourself. Then record what really happened.

Where the numbers come from

Closing prices are stored once or twice a day from a public price feed and checked before use: right currency and unit, no sudden jumps, not out of date. Every output has a "How this was calculated" section and a CSV, so you can check it yourself.

The strategies

Each strategy is a published method with a fixed set of rules, kept in the software and not editable. Where a fund differs from the original, the page says so. Strategy names belong to their authors; Wealtharr is not affiliated with them. Past results are not a guide to future results, and the Study tab is hypothetical history, not your results.
