How the study works
  • 9Sig starts 60% growth (LQQ3) / 40% safe (ERNS) and runs the live quarterly rule at each quarter end: the 9% signal line, half of new money, the large-buy throttle, 30 Down (from SPY), the 100%-up reset and 60/40 resets.
  • Monthly top-ups arrive on the first trading day of each month. In 9Sig they wait as cash for the next quarterly review; in the others they're invested that day.
  • All values are in pounds. SPY, VTI and QQQ are converted with daily GBP/USD rates and include reinvested dividends. Trades fill at that day's close with no dealing costs or tax.
  • Rebuilt history is an estimate: real leveraged funds also pay borrowing and swap costs, so early periods may look better than reality.
